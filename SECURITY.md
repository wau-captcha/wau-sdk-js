# Security policy

Please do not report security problems in public issues.

Write to **waucaptcha.team@gmail.com** with what you found, the steps to reproduce it and what it lets
an attacker do. We answer within 7 days.

What is in scope, what is not, and our promise not to take legal action against good-faith
research are on https://waucaptcha.com/security. The same contact is published in
https://waucaptcha.com/.well-known/security.txt.

The packages of this repository are in scope: `@waucaptcha-sdk/client`, `@waucaptcha-sdk/react` and
`@waucaptcha-sdk/server`.
